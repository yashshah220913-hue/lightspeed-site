DoseOn Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

DoseOn is a medication reminder app. This policy explains what data the app handles and — more importantly — what it doesn't.

The short version

Your data never leaves your phone. DoseOn stores everything on your device and does not collect, upload, share, or sell anything.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your name (used only for the in-app greeting)
  • The medications you add: names, schedules, and dose times
  • Your dose history: which doses you marked taken, skipped, or missed
  • Your reminder preferences: alarm sound, snooze time, vibration, and reminder style

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your device only. No account is required, and none of it is transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. DoseOn has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. The app works entirely offline.

What we share with third parties

Nothing. No data is shared with anyone, because no data ever reaches us in the first place.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to show dose reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarms — to ring at the exact time a dose is due.
  • Full-screen notifications — to show the alarm screen when a dose alarm rings.
  • Vibration and wake lock — so alarms can vibrate and wake the screen.
  • Run at startup — to re-register your alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service — to keep an alarm ringing until you respond to it.

These permissions are used solely to deliver reminders on your own device. They do not give the app access to any personal data.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, you are in full control. Deleting a medication removes its future reminders. To erase everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings (Apps → DoseOn → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app.

Children

DoseOn is intended for adults (18+) managing their own medications.

Changes to this policy

If DoseOn ever changes how it handles data — for example, by adding an optional backup feature — this policy will be updated first, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
