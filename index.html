How we build

Every app we ship follows the same few rules.

Focused

One clear purpose per app. No feature bloat, no settings you'll never touch.

Private by default

Wherever possible, your data stays on your device. No accounts unless the app truly needs one.

Honest

No dark patterns, no surprise charges, no tracking you didn't agree to.

Built for everyone

Large touch targets, readable type, and guided setup so the app works for all ages.

DoseOn Android

Pill reminder & medication alarm

A medication reminder that rings loud, even on silent mode, and takes over the screen so a dose can't be missed. Built for people and caregivers who need something harder to ignore than a notification.

  • Full-screen alarms that ring through silent and Do Not Disturb
  • Per-dose tracking and a monthly adherence calendar
  • Flexible schedules: daily, weekdays, or every N days
  • 100% offline. No account, no cloud, no ads.
